⚡ Load-Shedding Survival Guide

Study smarter during load-shedding with these campus resources.

📍 Powered Study Spots

  • Ferdinand Postma Library (generator backup)
  • Fanie Ferreira Student Centre
  • Engineering Building — computer labs
  • Business School Foyer

📶 Wi-Fi Hotspots

  • Library — full Wi-Fi during outages
  • Student Centre ground floor
  • Main gate security booth area

💡 Tips

  • Download lecture slides before outages
  • Use the EskomSePush app for your area schedule
  • Charge all devices during Stage 0 periods
  • eFundi assignments: request extensions proactively

📍 Powered Study Spots

  • Main Campus Library
  • Administration Block — lobby area
  • Law Faculty computer lab

📶 Wi-Fi Hotspots

  • Library entrance — reliable signal
  • Student Union building
  • Cafeteria area (limited hours)

💡 Tips

  • Mahikeng uses Eskom Zone 4 — check schedule
  • Some areas have 4–6 hour outages
  • Power banks highly recommended
  • Group study in library for shared resources

📍 Powered Study Spots

  • Vaal Campus Library (generator)
  • Ebenhaeser Building computer labs
  • Engineering Block — 24hr lab
  • Student Cafeteria (backup power)

📶 Wi-Fi Hotspots

  • Library — full campus Wi-Fi backup
  • Engineering courtyard
  • Vaal Mall nearby (1.5km) — free Wi-Fi

💡 Tips

  • Vanderbijlpark often in Zone 2 or 6
  • Industrial area means some grid priority
  • Use offline study apps: Anki, Notion
  • Check with residence office for generator hours
